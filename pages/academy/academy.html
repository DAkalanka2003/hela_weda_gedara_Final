Traditional Medicine Course

Comprehensive 3-month course designed to prepare you for the Traditional Medicine Examination. Guided by master native teachers to prepare you for state-level certifications.

Duration

3 Months (Weekend classes only - 6 hours per day)

Total Hours

72 Hours (Theory: 40 hours | Practical: 32 hours)

Syllabus Overview & Details

1. Theory Content (40 Hours)

  • Anatomy and Physiology (6 hours): Structural and functional overview of the human body.
  • Traditional Medicine Principles: Core native philosophical foundations and pathological concepts.
  • Herbal Properties & Usages: Detailed identification, properties, and medicinal applications of plants.
  • Safety & Clinical Hygiene: Sanitation practices and safe administration guidelines.
  • Basic Pathology: Diagnostic indicators and traditional disease classification.
  • Fractures & Dislocations Theory: Basic concepts of skeletal trauma management.

2. Practical Content (32 Hours)

  • Medicine Preparation: Hands-on formulation of:
    • Traditional warm herbal oils
    • Mellum (medicinal herbal paste)
    • Paththu (poultice wrap applications)
    • Peni Paththu (honey-based thick preparations)
    • Thelkira (specialized oil-infused mixtures)
  • Clinical Skills: Practical sessions in:
    • Medicinal herb identification and harvesting
    • Splinting and bandaging techniques
    • Traditional bone-setting and skeletal alignment for fractures
    • Joint dislocation and subluxation reduction treatments

Course Objectives

  • Fully prepare candidates for the government Traditional Medicine Examination.
  • Equip students with the practical competency to identify plants and formulate core remedies safely.
  • Inculcate safe clinical guidelines, ethics, and traditional treatment applications.

Traditional Orthopedic Medicine Course

Learn ancient Sri Lankan bone-setting techniques and traditional methods for managing fractures, dislocations, and musculoskeletal injuries. Tailored specifically for international practitioners, therapists, and alternative medicine enthusiasts.

Duration

2 Weekends (4 Days) - 6 Hours Daily (Total 24 Hours)

Format

Theory (2 hours daily) + Intensive Practical (4 hours daily)

Target Audience

This course is designed for:

Ayurvedic Practitioners
Wellness Professionals
Physiotherapists
Alternative Medicine Fans
Medical Students

Detailed Day-by-Day Syllabus

Day 1: Introduction & Foundations

Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

  • History and evolution of traditional orthopedic medicine in Sri Lanka.
  • Key philosophical foundations (Deshiya Chikitsa and Ayurvedic influence).
  • Overview of common musculoskeletal conditions treated traditionally.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

  • Traditional perspective on the identification of bones and joints.
  • Basic diagnostic techniques without modern imaging devices.
  • Hands-on training: traditional pulse reading (Nadi Vignana) & observational diagnostics.
  • Botanical identification walk or interactive herb display session.

Day 2: Herbal Preparations & External Therapies

Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

  • Introduction to key herbs, oils, and pastes utilized in orthopedic treatments.
  • Scientific methods of traditional medicine preparation: Decoctions, poultices, and oils.
  • Contraindications and safety guidelines.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

  • Hands-on formulation: preparing traditional warm herbal oils.
  • Techniques for application of herbal poultices (Paththu).
  • Traditional massage strokes and techniques designed for immediate pain relief.

Day 3: Bone-Setting & Fracture Management

Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

  • Core principles of bone-setting in Sri Lankan traditional practice.
  • Critical comparisons: traditional vs. modern fracture management.
  • Professional ethics, referrals, and legal aspects of practicing traditional medicine.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

  • Splinting techniques using bamboo, specific barks, cloths, and natural bindings.
  • Traditional joint resetting techniques for common skeletal dislocations.
  • Bandaging methods, customized slings, and wrap setups.

Day 4: Sprains, Soft Tissue Injuries & Rehabilitation

Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

  • Soft tissue injuries management: sprains, muscle strains, and contusions.
  • Nutritional guidelines, rest, and lifestyle choices during orthopedic recovery.
  • Long-term rehabilitation planning and future injury prevention strategies.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

  • Preparation and application of traditional medicated steam therapies.
  • Rehabilitative massage routines and mild mobilization movements.
  • Participant evaluations and practical competency demonstrations.
  • Final wrap-up Q&A and course feedback.
