Traditional Medicine Course

Comprehensive 3-month course designed to prepare you for the Traditional Medicine Examination. Guided by master native teachers to prepare you for state-level certifications.

Duration

3 Months (Weekend classes only)

Total Hours

72 Hours (Theory: 40 hours | Practical: 32 hours)

Syllabus Overview

1. Theory Content (40 Hours)

  • Anatomy and Physiology (6 hours)
  • Traditional medicine core principles & pathological concepts
  • Herbal properties, identification, and usages
  • Safety and clinical hygiene

2. Practical Content (32 Hours)

  • Formulation of traditional oils, Mellum, Paththu, Peni Paththu, and Thelkira.
  • Clinical skills: Plant identification, bandaging techniques, bone-setting, joint subluxation and dislocation treatment.

1st Installment: LKR 25,000 (At registration)

2nd Installment: LKR 25,000 (Before practicals)

Traditional Orthopedic Medicine Course

Learn ancient Sri Lankan bone-setting techniques and traditional methods for managing fractures, dislocations, and musculoskeletal injuries. Tailored for practitioners and therapists globally.

Duration

2 Weekends (4 Days) - 6 Hours Daily

Format

Theory + Intensive Practical

Detailed Syllabus Breakdown

Day 1: Introduction & Foundations

History of traditional Sri Lankan orthopedic medicine, pulse reading (Nadi Vignana), and botanical identification walks.

Day 2: Herbal Oils & Pastes

Formulation of warm herbal oils, preparation of poultices (Paththu), and soft tissue massage techniques for immediate pain relief.

Day 3: Bone Setting & Dislocations

Traditional bone alignment, joint reduction, and custom splints using local plants, bamboo, and linen bindings.

Day 4: Rehabilitation & Steam Therapy

Medicated steam treatments, mobilization regimes, diet, and long-term preventative care. Final assessments and certificate presentation.
