Traditional Orthopedic Medicine Course

Learn ancient Sri Lankan bone-setting techniques and traditional methods for managing fractures, dislocations, and musculoskeletal injuries.

Theory (8 Hours) Practical (16 Hours)

Course Fee: $350 USD

Course Objectives

  • Gain deep theoretical knowledge on Deshiya Chikitsa bone-setting principles.
  • Develop practical competency in preparing and applying herbal oils/poultices.
  • Understand client safety, contraindications, and emergency parameters.
  • Earn a recognized certificate from a traditional Ayurvedic healing center.

Target Audience

  • Ayurvedic practitioners
  • Wellness professionals
  • Alternative medicine fans
  • Physiotherapists
  • Medical students

Course Details

  • Duration: 2 Weekends (4 Days)
  • Schedule: 6 Hours Daily (Total 24 Hours)
  • Format: Theory + Practical sessions
  • Language: English

Detailed Day-by-Day Course Outline

Day 1: Introduction & Foundations
Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

History and evolution of traditional orthopedic medicine in Sri Lanka. Key philosophical foundations of Deshiya Chikitsa.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

Observational diagnostics, traditional pulse reading (Nadi Vignana), and interactive botanical identification walk.

Day 2: Herbal Preparations & External Therapies
Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

Introduction to key herbs, oils and pastes utilized in orthopedic treatments. Formulation guidelines and clinical safety parameters.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

Hands-on formulation: preparing traditional warm herbal oils, applying poultices (Paththu), and soft tissue massage strokes.

Day 3: Bone-Setting & Fracture Management
Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

Core principles of bone-setting, comparative analysis of traditional vs. modern fracture management, and professional clinical ethics.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

Splinting techniques using bamboo and specific barks. Traditional joint resetting and reduction, and bandaging methods.

Day 4: Soft Tissue Injuries & Rehabilitation
Morning Theory Session (2 Hours)

Soft tissue injuries management (sprains, strains). Nutritional guidelines, rest parameters, and long-term recovery planning.

Afternoon Practical Session (4 Hours)

Medicated steam therapies, rehabilitative massage routines, participant evaluations, and competency demonstrations.
