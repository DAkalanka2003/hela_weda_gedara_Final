Professional Ayurvedic Assessment

Consultations at Weda Gedara involve an in-depth evaluation of your body's bio-energies (Doshas). Our native practitioners employ traditional diagnostics like pulse checks, tongue mapping, and physical observation to locate toxins and functional imbalances.
