Leaders in Authentic Ayurvedic Healing

Hela Weda Gedara is the premier Ayurvedic center in Galle Boossa, whose comprehensive program includes everything needed to fully understand and apply the Ayurvedic approach towards health. We believe health is not just the absence of disease, but a state of emotional, psychological and spiritual wholeness.

Holistic Treatment: Complete natural healing approach restoring bio-physiology to its original state.
Expert Consultation: Comprehensive consultation with qualified physicians for personalized care.
Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Under the concept of "Wellness is the Greatest Wealth," we aim to preserve the excellent heritage of Sri Lankan indigenous Hela Wedakama, utilizing its knowledge to spread internationally and create a healthy Sri Lankan society for future generations.

Our Mission

To preserve the Sri Lankan heritage of Hela Wedakama while creating necessary herbal gardens for its practice, nourishing modern society with knowledge, and providing essential treatments using both indigenous and international methods, incorporating modern technology.
